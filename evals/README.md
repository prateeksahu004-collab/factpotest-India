# Evaluation methodology

All results in `evals/results/` are produced by `scripts/run_evals.py` and are committed so they can be
reproduced and diffed when prompts or models change (prompt versions live in `factprotest/prompts.py`).

## Gold claim set (`gold_claims.jsonl`)

* **Source**: 39 passages sampled from the Wikipedia knowledge base (4 per protest, preferring passages with
  numbers or dates, excluding article introductions).
* **Drafting**: `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` wrote three claims per passage: one **SUPPORTED** paraphrase, one **REFUTED**
  single-detail perturbation (number, date, person, place or outcome) and one **NOT_ENOUGH_INFO** claim the
  passage neither confirms nor denies. A different model family from the verifier (gpt-oss) was used on purpose
  to reduce self-agreement bias.
* **Review**: every one of the 117 drafts was checked against its source passage and, for NOT_ENOUGH_INFO claims,
  searched across the whole knowledge base. 12 were removed (`gold_review_log.json` records each reason):
  perturbations that were still logically true (e.g. "more than 50 trains" when the source says "more than 350"),
  meta-statements about the passage, and NEI claims that another Wikipedia passage actually covers.
* **Final set**: 105 claims: 39 SUPPORTED, 36 REFUTED, 30 NOT_ENOUGH_INFO, across all 10 protests.

## Suites

| Suite | What it measures | LLM calls |
|---|---|---|
| `retrieval` | Recall@k and MRR of the gold passage for BM25, dense (bge-small) and hybrid RRF retrieval | none |
| `paraphrase` | The same, with gold claims heavily paraphrased (new vocabulary, same facts), mimicking how citizens actually write | Qwen paraphrases, cached |
| `verify` | Verdict accuracy, macro-F1, per-class precision/recall and confusion matrix for several retrieval × model configurations, plus an *oracle* configuration that is given the gold passage, which separates reasoning errors from retrieval errors | yes |
| `endtoend` | On the 424-opinion synthetic corpus: recall of injected false claims, false-alarm rate on accurate and fact-free opinions, ROC-AUC of the final score for accurate vs false opinions, LLM zero-shot stance accuracy, and a stance-bias audit of the score (Kruskal–Wallis, Mann–Whitney, rank-biserial r) | none (uses stored assessments) |
| `consistency` | Run-to-run variation of the final score and the predicted stance when the same opinions are re-scored | yes |

`scripts/train_models.py` adds leave-one-protest-out cross-validation for the stance classifiers and a threshold
sweep for the coordinated-campaign detector (`artifacts/metrics/`).

## Stance label audit (`results/stance_label_audit.json`)

A manual audit of 30 random corpus opinions showed the generator often labelled stance toward the *policy* rather than
the *protest*. The 21 clear-cut audit cases became a check set; ambiguous ones were excluded. All 400 organic opinions
were re-annotated by Qwen with each protest's explicit demand (`scripts/relabel_stance.py`): 95% agreement with the audit,
Cohen's κ = 0.87, against 57% for the generator's own labels. The original labels are kept as `stance_generator`.
`run_evals.py stance` then measures gpt-oss-20b zero-shot with the same target-aware definition.

## Known limitations

* The corpus is synthetic: generated by `gpt-oss-120b` from persona specs. Stance labels were repaired (above), but
  "accurate_fact" / "false_fact" modes rely on the generator following instructions; error analysis found accurate
  opinions that use the announcement date of the farm-law repeal as the repeal date. Treat absolute numbers as indicative.
* Only the first 100 corpus opinions (protests 1–3) are fully scored so far: the free tier caps gpt-oss-120b at 200K
  tokens/day. `score_corpus.py` resumes where it stopped.
* Exact-chunk retrieval recall is a lower bound because passages overlap by ~35 words; `relaxed_recall` counts the
  gold passage's neighbours as hits.
* The corpus generator and the production verifier share a model family (gpt-oss), so end-to-end misinformation
  recall may be optimistic. The gold claim set avoids this by using Qwen.
* Free-tier rate limits (8K tokens/min per model) bound throughput; reported latencies exclude queueing.
